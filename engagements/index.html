How I work with leaders and organizations

I work with organizations that are serious about moving AI from experimentation to measurable impact. The form of our collaboration can vary, but the focus remains constant: clarity of intent, operating model design, and execution that holds up under real enterprise constraints.

Advisory & leadership roles

I engage with leadership teams in advisory, fractional, or full-time roles where AI transformation requires sustained ownership rather than isolated initiatives.

  • Designing AI operating models that scale beyond pilots
  • Clarifying governance, decision rights, and accountability
  • Aligning AI ambition with organizational reality
  • Translating strategy into execution mechanisms

Keynotes & leadership sessions

I deliver keynotes and leadership sessions for executive and senior audiences who want clarity rather than hype. These sessions focus on framing AI as an operating model challenge, not a tooling exercise.

Audiences typically leave with sharper mental models, clearer language, and a more grounded basis for decision-making.

Workshops

Workshops are designed to turn intent into action. They are hands-on, context-aware, and grounded in enterprise realities.

  • AI adoption in real workflows
  • Operating model and governance design

  • Moving from pilots to repeatable execution

Who this is for

My work is most relevant for leaders operating in large or complex organizations where scale, cost, governance, and risk matter.
