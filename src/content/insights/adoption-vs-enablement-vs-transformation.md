Most organizations talk about “AI transformation” when they’re actually doing one of two other things: **adoption** or **enablement**. The distinction matters because each one has a different goal, different failure modes, and different measurements.

## 1) The three questions (the simplest model)

- **AI Adoption:** Are people using AI? 【1-23a183】  
- **AI Enablement:** Can people use AI effectively? 【1-23a183】【2-27dc01】  
- **AI Transformation:** Is the organization redesigned around AI? 【1-23a183】【2-27dc01】  

If you don’t know which question you’re answering, you can’t pick the right interventions.

## 2) What each one focuses on

### AI Adoption
Adoption is observable behavior change and usage in real workflows. 【2-27dc01】  
If adoption is isolated, you often get “local productivity only” without systemic value. 【1-23a183】  

### AI Enablement
Enablement is the set of capabilities that make effective adoption possible: data readiness, system integration, governance, skills, and cultural conditions. 【2-27dc01】  
If enablement is isolated, you get “capability without impact.” 【1-23a183】  

### AI Transformation
Transformation is the deliberate restructuring of operating model, governance, decision rights, and workflows to enable AI-driven value at scale. 【2-27dc01】  
If transformation is isolated, you risk “high cost and complexity.” 【1-23a183】  

## 3) Practical measurement (what I actually look at)

A simple way to stay honest is to measure each layer separately:

- **Adoption:** who uses AI, how often, and in which workflows. 【2-27dc01】  
- **Enablement:** are the conditions in place (governance, integration, skills) to sustain adoption. 【2-27dc01】  
- **Transformation:** are decision rights and workflows materially redesigned so AI changes how value is created. 【2-27dc01】  

## 4) Common confusion patterns

- “We rolled out licenses” → enablement activity, not adoption. 【2-27dc01】  
- “Many people use AI” → adoption, not transformation. 【2-27dc01】  
- “We built a platform” → enablement, not value by default. 【1-23a183】  

## 5) The punchline

Treat these as **three different management systems**.  
When you align terminology to the real work, your execution gets faster and your measurement stops lying.
