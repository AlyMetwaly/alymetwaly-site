Governance is often treated as paperwork. In practice, it is the **interface between speed and risk**: who can ship, what evidence is required, and how teams learn as usage grows.

This note outlines how I think about enterprise AI governance as an operating model problem—aligned incentives, clear accountability, and lightweight controls that compound over time rather than blocking every experiment.
