What I help leaders get right about AI

I work with organizations that are serious about moving AI from experimentation to measurable impact. My focus is not on tools in isolation, but on the operating models, decision structures, and ways of working that determine whether AI actually changes outcomes.

Much of today’s AI frustration comes from mixing three different challenges: adoption, enablement, and transformation. I help leadership teams separate these clearly, decide what they are actually trying to achieve, and then execute accordingly.

How I typically help

Engagements often center on one or more of the following:

  • Designing AI operating models that scale beyond pilots and siloed teams
  • Driving real adoption of AI in day-to-day workflows, not shelfware
  • Clarifying governance, decision rights, and accountability for AI
  • Defining success metrics that connect adoption to business value

Engagement formats

Depending on context and need, I work in several formats:

  • Full-time or fractional leadership roles in AI transformation
  • Executive advisory engagements and strategic sparring
  • Keynotes and leadership sessions on AI transformation and operating models
  • Hands-on workshops focused on adoption, governance, and execution

Who this is for

My work is most relevant for leaders operating in large, complex organizations where scale, governance, cost, and risk matter. I typically partner with CIOs, CTOs, transformation leaders, and business executives who want clarity and execution rather than hype.
