The false tradeoff in enterprise AI: move fast and accept risk, or govern heavily and accept delay.

Both fail. Fast without guardrails produces rework and reputational exposure. Heavy governance produces shadow AI and workarounds that auditors discover later.

## Governance as transmission, not brake

Good governance answers three questions quickly:

- **What is allowed** by default—and what requires review?
- **Who decides** when a use case sits in the gray zone?
- **How fast** does a decision get made, measured in days—not quarters?

If your governance model can't answer those in one page, it's too heavy for the pace AI demands.

## Design for reversibility

Reversible bets with clear rollback criteria move faster than perfect plans. Tier your risk: low-friction paths for bounded experiments, structured review only when customer data, revenue paths, or regulatory exposure are in play.

Speed and control compound when the operating model is explicit—not when every team invents its own rules.
