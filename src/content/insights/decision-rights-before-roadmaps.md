Most AI roadmaps fail before the first milestone—not because the technology is wrong, but because **nobody can say yes**.

## The pattern

Leadership approves a transformation narrative. Product, engineering, and business lines each interpret it differently. Every cross-functional decision becomes a negotiation. Pilots multiply. Nothing compounds.

The operating model was never defined.

## What decision rights actually mean

Four questions, answered explicitly:

- **Who funds** AI bets—and at what threshold?
- **Who approves** workflow changes that touch customer or revenue paths?
- **Who owns** adoption metrics—not just platform uptime?
- **Who escalates** when governance and speed collide?

Without answers, you get consensus theater: meetings that produce alignment slides, not accountable next steps.

## The fix is structural, not motivational

Don't workshop your way to clarity. Document decision rights, tie them to a weekly leadership rhythm, and measure whether decisions actually get made inside the cadence.

Roadmaps follow operating models. Not the other way around.
